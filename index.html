PECA Compliance & Pakistan Data Protection Agreement

🛡️ Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA 2016) Notice:

By using this application, you agree to comply with PECA 2016 regulations. Any unauthorized access, spoofing of GPS/telemetry data, vehicle speed cheating on leaderboards, or harassment of nearby users via the Private Radar chat is strictly prohibited and subject to account suspension and reporting.

📍 GPS Tracking & Social Radar Consent:

islorun reads your location to track distance, average pace, and draw routes on the photo studio badges. Your approximate relative bearing is shared with nearby runners on the Radar view. You can activate "Ghost Mode" at any time in the Radar tab to hide your position completely.

💳 SadaBiz & Payments Terms:

All premium purchases are billed manually via SadaBiz invoices. TRX ID reference codes entered during verification are matched against audit webhooks. Attempts to submit fake TRX IDs will flag your account.

🚦 Outdoor Running Traffic Safety:

Do not focus on the app interface while running or cycling in traffic. Keep eyes on the road and stay aware of your surroundings, specifically when navigating trail courses or major roadways in Islamabad.
